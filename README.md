# Brandex Community Platform

Welcome to the **Brandex Community Platform** repository. This is the official digital showcase and education platform for Brandex's emerging technology communities, school workshops, research initiatives, and live events.

## Features

- **Education Pathways**: Discover school & youth programs (Geniusphere series), college initiatives, professional skills, and hands-on buildathons.
- **Community Circles**: Join specialized technology circles in Artificial Intelligence, Zero-Trust Cybersecurity, and Swiss Editorial UX.
- **Training Catalog**: Browse rigorous, cohort-based courses designed to elevate technical expertise.
- **Brand Ambassador Program**: Campus leadership program for student leads and faculty sponsors.
- **Events & Summits**: View upcoming technical summits, capture-the-flag wargames, and live coding labs.
- **Stories & Impact**: Real narratives of student achievements and community milestones.

## 90-Day Launch Plan & Execution Strategy

### Goal
Build a small, high-quality founder/builder community in Bangalore first, then expand once the format works.

### North Star Metrics
- **50 to 100** qualified members
- **20** founding members
- **3** in-person events
- **2** online sessions
- **10+** meaningful introductions/collaborations
- **1** clear repeatable community format

### Phased Roadmap

#### Phase 1: Days 1 to 15 - Define and set up
- Decide the exact member profile: founders, operators, designers, devs, indie builders.
- Write the community promise in one line.
- Build landing page on `brandex.co.in` with:
  - What Brandex is & who it's for
  - Why join
  - Waitlist form & founder application form
- Create a short screening form so the community stays high-quality.
- Set up WhatsApp/Discord for early members and notion/CRM for applicants.
- Publish launch content explaining why Brandex exists.

#### Phase 2: Days 16 to 30 - Recruit founding members
- Personally invite 30 to 50 curated people.
- Select 15 to 20 founding members.
- Finalize first community rituals: monthly founder meetup, weekly online check-in, demo/share session, peer intro loop.

#### Phase 3: Days 31 to 45 - Run the first event
- Host one small offline meetup (15 to 25 people max).
- Agenda: short intro, founder lightning intros, honest founder story, problem-sharing round, networking block.
- Capture photos, quotes, 3 short member stories, 1 recap post.

#### Phase 4: Days 46 to 60 - Build repeatability
- Run 1 online session and test "Founder of the Week" or "What I'm building" formats.
- Start referral loop (founding members invite 1 person with manual approval).

#### Phase 5: Days 61 to 75 - Deepen the community
- Launch small peer pods of 4 to 6 people matched by interest.
- Introduce recurring rituals: Monday wins, Friday builds, monthly demo day.

#### Phase 6: Days 76 to 90 - Scale carefully
- Host second offline event.
- Finalize membership pipeline: application -> review -> invite -> onboarding -> first event.
